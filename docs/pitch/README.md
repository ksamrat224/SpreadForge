## One-line pitch

> SpreadForge is a Solana trading playground where users learn, practice, compete, and experiment with social trading without risking real money.

## Thirty-second pitch

> Most trading education is either passive or disconnected from verifiable results. SpreadForge gives users an interactive place to test market-making strategies, paper trade with live prices, understand their portfolio, and compare results publicly. Solana stores the durable portfolio and final scores, while MagicBlock handles fast trading sessions. Everything currently runs with simulated assets on devnet, so users can learn without putting real funds at risk.

## The story to repeat

SpreadForge has four simple ideas:

1. Learn through deterministic market scenarios.
2. Practice against live market information.
3. Prove results on-chain instead of trusting browser calculations.
4. Explore collaborative trading with transparent rules and simulated funds.

## Presenter reminder

Do not describe SpreadForge as an investment product or prediction engine. It is an educational simulation. Momentum labels describe recent movement; they do not predict future prices.
